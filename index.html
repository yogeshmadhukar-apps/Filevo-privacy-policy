Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Filevo ("Filevo", "we", "us", or "our") is a document and image conversion application developed by Madhukar & Sons.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Filevo Android application.

Privacy-first processing: Filevo is designed to process your documents and images on your device. Your files are not uploaded to Filevo servers for conversion.

2. Information We Collect

Information processed by Filevo

Filevo does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, or other personal information to use the core conversion features.

Files that you select for conversion, compression, resizing, extraction, or other supported operations are processed locally on your device whenever the applicable feature supports on-device processing.

Filevo does not intentionally upload the contents of your documents or images to its own servers.

3. File and Document Privacy

Filevo may request access to files that you explicitly select through the Android file picker or other supported Android mechanisms.

Filevo uses the selected files only to perform the operation requested by you, such as conversion, compression, resizing, merging, splitting, or extraction.

  • We do not sell your files.
  • We do not share your file contents with advertisers.
  • We do not intentionally upload your selected files to Filevo servers.
  • You control which files are selected for processing.

4. Advertising

Filevo is supported by advertising. We may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements within the application.

Advertising providers may collect or process certain information, which may include device information, application information, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and information about advertising interactions, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Filevo does not provide advertisers with the contents of your documents or images for advertising purposes.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Android Permissions

Filevo may use certain Android capabilities that are necessary for application functionality.

  • Internet: Used for advertising services and other network-related functionality where required.
  • File access: Used when you select files for conversion or when saving generated files.
  • Vibration: May be used for supported user-interface interactions.

Filevo does not request broad access to your entire personal media library when it is not required. File selection is performed through supported Android file-access mechanisms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the application and its data handling.

Because Filevo is designed for on-device processing, selected files generally remain on your device during supported conversion operations.

However, no software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Filevo does not maintain a Filevo-operated cloud database containing your uploaded documents or images.

Files created by Filevo may remain on your device until you delete them using your device's file-management facilities or the relevant functionality provided by the application.

8. Third-Party Services

Filevo may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services
  • Other Android platform services required for application functionality

These third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Filevo is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to provide consent for online services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Filevo.

10. Your Choices

You control the files you select for processing and can remove generated files from your device.

You can also manage Android permissions through your device settings, where applicable.

Advertising-related controls may also be available through your Android device and Google account settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Filevo, applicable laws, or third-party services.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
